CORRESPONDING SOURCE
Perplex-FFmpeg-Source-1.1.6.zip contains the exact FFmpeg, x264 and LAME input
archives, MinGW-w64 source and Debian patches, license texts, build script,
configuration, and toolchain identification. Its checksums and executable
mapping are recorded in FFMPEG-MANIFEST.json. See FFMPEG-SOURCE.txt.
No proprietary or nonfree media codec library is included.

Media processing and installation work offline. Nothing is downloaded by
the installer or uploaded by the converter. The website and license links
open in the user's browser only when selected.
